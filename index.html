About

I'm a cybersecurity student who would rather find out how something actually works than accept an explanation at face value. I learn by testing, breaking and debugging, then working out why. Digital forensics is my strongest area, backed by a real malware case, podium finishes in CTFs and web application testing. I lead the blue team at my college's Cyber Security Club and I explain findings to people who aren't technical.
